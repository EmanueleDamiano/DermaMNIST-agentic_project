          Agents and models

          Without an LLM the answer is the models' weighted vote. A small local LLM may take a few minutes.
          A model different from the tester's makes the review more independent. Without an LLM the automatic checks remain.
          Proposes the runs and analyses the curves. Without an LLM a deterministic policy decides (plateau → lr ×0.3, overfitting → more weight decay).

          New training campaign

          This is the training agent's goal. Before training anything it shows you the plan, which you can edit.
          "Agent decides": starts from FPViT and may propose another, with a reason.
          You always approve the plan and the promotion into the ensemble.
          Then the agent stops and proposes what to change.
